What these mockups propose
A “Needs you” card
The four collapsibles hide whether anything is outstanding. One card names what is waiting, and the tabs carry counts so gaps are visible without tapping.
Gaps as first-class rows
A missing return leg or an unbooked night in Ravello shows as a dashed amber row where it belongs, not as an absence you have to notice.
Import shows its working
Each parsed booking previews exactly what it will create before you accept it, and says plainly when it could not read the mail.
A day view
Travel, stay, eat and experience already share a timeline in the data. The itinerary screen draws it, and empty days become an invitation.
