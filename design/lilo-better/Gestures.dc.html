Why the fallback column exists
A swipe is invisible, unlabelled and unavailable to anyone using VoiceOver or Switch Control. Apple’s own guidance is that a gesture accelerates a task; it never owns one. If a row can only be actioned by swiping, that action does not exist for some of your group.
Where gestures earn their keep here
Repetition. Voting on six places, ticking off a packing list, chasing three people for money — these are the same action done many times, which is exactly when reaching for a menu each time starts to hurt. One-off actions keep their buttons.
What is deliberately not a gesture
Paying, settling and cancelling a trip. Anything that moves money or cannot be undone by a toast keeps a labelled button and a moment of friction. Swipe-to-delete on a whole trip is how people lose a holiday.
