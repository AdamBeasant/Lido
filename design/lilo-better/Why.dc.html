Lilo
Four sources, checked
Twenty-six screens, every one verified by app name before use. An earlier round leaked in Phantom, Brave, Partiful and GroupMe; none of them are here.
Luma 6+3 flows verified
What it actually does
The event page is one ambient-tinted scroll: cover card, title, a four-tile action row, then labelled sections — Where with a map and the local temperature, Who’s coming as three counts, Blasts from the host, and a locked Manage grid. Creation is cover-first with rows edited in place.
What Lilo takes
The trip page, near enough wholesale. Going / Asked / Can’t as three counts. Shouts. A Manage grid for the organiser. Creation with the picker opening over its own row.
Wise 11 verified
What it actually does
Jars: a named pot with its own balance and its own circular actions. A total is never shown without the rows that built it — itemised lines, a dashed rule, the net figure biggest. Actions are green circles with labels beneath.
What Lilo takes
The kitty is a jar. Settle up is the breakdown sheet, all-caps heading and dashed total included. Add / Settle / Request are the circles.
Family 8 verified
What it actually does
Lists with no cards and no dividers. The section switch shares its row with the total, so the number sits at the end of a control rather than in a hero. Icon-only tab bar, one circular add button, one full-width pill when there is a single thing to do.
What Lilo takes
The home screen: Trips / Memories with the next countdown at the end of the row, then three trips and a lot of black.
Grok 11 verified
What it actually does
One surface, two modes as plain text. A collapsed Thoughts row stands in for the reasoning. Answers are headed prose with bullets, not bubbles, and carry an action row underneath. The composer floats as a dark block with its controls inside it.
What Lilo takes
Add and Ask. The reading step before a parse. Answers that lead with the number. The composer with attach, scope pill, mic and send inside it.
What the four have in common, and what Lilo does with it
A screen answers one question
Wise opens on a balance, Family on a list, Luma on an event. None of them opens on navigation. So the trip page opens on the trip, and the five tabs are gone.
Numbers carry the meaning
Guest counts, balances, days remaining. All four set the number large and the label small. Lilo has more numbers than it shows — who has paid, who has packed, who has flights.
One place to act
A four-tile row, a circle of actions, a composer. Never a menu that asks what kind of thing you are about to make.
Dark is mine
Family ships light and dark. Wise and Grok are light with dark variants. Only Luma’s event page is reliably dark, and it earns it by tinting itself from the cover art.
I have followed Luma here because a trip is mostly photographs. On black a cover stops being decoration. It is a judgement, not a citation, and a light mode still has to exist.
