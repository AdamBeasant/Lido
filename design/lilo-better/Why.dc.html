Lilo
Four sources, checked
Thirty-two screens, each verified by app name. There is no separate “Grok Bot” in Mobbin’s index — every result for it returns Grok.
Luma9+3 flows verified
What it does
The event page is one scroll tinted from its own cover: cover card, a four-tile action row, then labelled sections — Where with a map and the local temperature, Who’s coming as three counts, Blasts from the host, and a Manage grid behind a lock. Creation is cover-first with the picker opening over its own row.
What Lilo takes
The trip page and Manage, near enough wholesale. Going / Asked / Can’t. Shouts. The in-place date picker on New trip.
Wise11 verified
What it does
Jars: a named pot with its own balance and circular actions. A total is never shown without the rows that built it — itemised lines, a dashed rule, the net figure biggest. Actions are green circles with labels beneath.
What Lilo takes
The kitty is a jar. Settle up is the breakdown, all-caps heading and dashed total included.
Family8 verified
What it does
Lists with no cards and no dividers. The section switch shares its row with the total, so the number sits at the end of a control rather than in a hero. Icon-only tab bar, one circular add button, one pill when there is a single thing to do.
What Lilo takes
The home screen: Trips / Memories with the next countdown at the end of the row, then three trips and a lot of space.
Grok11 verified
What it does
One surface, two modes as plain text. A collapsed Thoughts row stands in for the reasoning. Answers open on a bold lead sentence, then prose and bullets with an action row and a model-and-time stamp beneath. The composer turns black with a Stop while generating.
What Lilo takes
Add and Ask. The reading step before a parse. Answers that lead with the number. The composer with attach, scope pill and send inside it.
What the four agree on
A screen answers one question
Wise opens on a balance, Family on a list, Luma on an event. None opens on navigation, so the trip page opens on the trip and the five tabs are gone.
Numbers carry the meaning
Guest counts, balances, days left. All four set the number large and the label small. Lilo knows more numbers than it shows.
One place to act
A tile row, a ring of circles, a composer. Never a menu asking what kind of thing you are about to make.
Light, as asked
Which is also where three of the four actually live. Only Luma’s event page is reliably dark, and its real trick is the tint, not the darkness — the cover’s own colours washed softly behind the content. That works on white, and it is what the trip pages do here.
