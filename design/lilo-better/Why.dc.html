iOS 26 Liquid Glass, and where it stops
Chrome floats, content does not
Nav is separate capsules rather than a bar, the tab bar is a floating pill beside its own add button, and the composer is a glass slab. Content stays flat and opaque so it never competes.
Two variants only
Regular over content, at 80–58% white with blur 28 and saturate 190. Clear over a photo, at 26–13% with a scrim beneath it. Both get a 0.5px hairline and an inset specular top edge.
Concentric radii
A 26px group holds 16px tiles at a 10px inset. Lists and cards stay on their own radii; glass never nests inside glass.
Worth knowing: glass over a light photograph loses contrast fast. Every clear-glass element here sits on a scrim, and the trip page keeps its title on plain ground rather than over the cover.
