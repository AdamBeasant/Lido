Three flows, counted
Screens are easy to judge and flows are not, so here are the three that matter with their step counts. Counted from the trip page; typing excluded.

Five tabs are how the app is built, not how a day is lived. The plan orders a day by time, so the villa, the table and the boat sit where they actually fall. Wise’s rule still holds: never a figure without the rows behind it, which is why a day shows its items rather than a count of them.
Undo, not confirm
Filing, deleting and un-voting happen at once and offer Undo for a minute. A modal before every small action only teaches people to dismiss modals.
Sheets keep context
Creating and adding happen in sheets over the trip, so the thing being described stays visible behind them. Only the trip itself is a full push.
One primary per screen
Exactly one filled button in view. If two things look equally important, neither reads as the answer.
Empty states do work
An empty day offers the saved place that fits it; an unbooked trip names the decision that is blocking. Nothing merely says “nothing here”.
